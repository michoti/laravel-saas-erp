## 2. About Page

### About Grafame ERP

#### Practical software for connected businesses

Grafame ERP is a SaaS business platform built to bring everyday operations into a more coherent system. We believe growing companies should be able to improve how work gets done without taking on a maze of disconnected applications or a disruptive, all-at-once transformation.

Our approach is modular and operational. Start with the processes that matter most, bring the relevant people and records together, and expand as your requirements become clearer. The result should feel less like software imposed on a business and more like a useful structure around the work already happening.

### Why we are building it

Small and growing organizations often reach a point where informal systems begin to slow them down. A spreadsheet may have been the right first step, but over time important details spread across files, messages, and individual routines. Teams then spend energy checking whether information is current instead of acting on it.

Grafame ERP is being developed to make core business activity easier to organize and review. The platform provides a tenant-based SaaS foundation, subscription and module management, and an operational POS area for products, customers, orders, payments, refunds, promotions, and stock activity. These foundations give businesses a place to build consistent routines and give the product room to expand through additional apps.

### What guides the product

#### Clarity before complexity

Useful software should make the next step easier to see. We aim to keep work organized around understandable records and processes, so teams can find the information they need and make decisions with less guesswork.

#### Progress in manageable stages

Changing business systems can feel risky when every process is tied together. A modular platform gives teams a more deliberate path: begin with a focused workflow, establish it, and widen the system when the business is ready.

#### Shared work, clear responsibility

Good operations depend on people knowing where work belongs. Grafame ERP is designed around organization-specific workspaces, with access and responsibilities managed in context. The intent is to help teams collaborate while keeping administration legible.

#### Reliable operational records

Business decisions are only as useful as the information behind them. Transaction and stock records should be connected to the activity they describe, making it easier to review what happened and what needs attention next.

### Who we serve

Grafame ERP is intended for businesses that want to coordinate sales and operational information, including teams managing products, customers, and daily transactions. Selected apps and configuration should reflect each organization's real processes and stage of growth.

### Our commitment

## 3. Apps Page

### Apps for the work behind your business

Grafame ERP is organized as a collection of business apps. Each app supports a distinct set of workflows, while the wider platform provides a shared place to manage your organization and its access. Select the capabilities that fit your current priorities. Availability varies by plan and release.

### Point of Sale

#### Make each transaction part of the bigger picture

The POS app supports day-to-day selling and transaction records. Staff can work with a product catalog, customer details, orders, payments, and refunds in a connected workflow. Sales can include relevant pricing, tax, discount, and promotion information, giving the business a clearer record of what was sold and how the transaction was handled.

Use POS to establish a repeatable checkout process, review order history, and keep sales activity tied to the products and customers involved. For businesses with stock-tracked products, recorded inventory movements provide a foundation for understanding stock changes and identifying low-stock items.

**Useful for:** Retail counters, in-person sales teams, and businesses that need a structured transaction record.

### Customer Relationship Management

#### Turn customer details into a more consistent relationship

CRM brings customer-facing work into an organized process. Build on customer records with a clearer view of conversations, opportunities, follow-ups, and service needs. Instead of relying on one person's memory, teams can develop shared routines for keeping relationships active.

Use CRM to define how your team captures customer context and decides what should happen next. A well-maintained relationship record can help staff prepare for a conversation, follow up at the right time, and deliver a more consistent experience across handoffs.

**Useful for:** Sales teams, account owners, and service teams coordinating ongoing customer relationships.

### Inventory Management

#### Know what is moving and what needs attention

Inventory workflows help your team organize product information and monitor stock activity. Track changes, review stock levels, and identify items that may need replenishment. A clear product record supports POS activity while giving operations a better basis for purchasing and planning decisions.

Inventory needs vary widely by business. Establish the rules that matter to your operation, including which products are stock-tracked and how staff record movements. Consistent input is the foundation for a useful view of availability.

**Useful for:** Businesses managing physical goods, replenishment routines, or stock-sensitive sales.

## 5. Documentation

### Grafame ERP User Guide

This documentation helps organization owners, managers, and staff understand the basic shape of Grafame ERP and complete common operational tasks. Your screens and permissions may differ depending on plan, configuration, and role. Use the navigation available in your account as the source of truth for enabled apps.

### 1. Understand your workspace

Grafame ERP is provided as a SaaS platform. Each customer organization works in its own tenant workspace, and platform administrators manage organization subscriptions and module access. Within your workspace, your role determines which areas and actions are available.

If an app or action is missing, first confirm that you are signed into the correct organization and account. Then ask your workspace owner whether the app is included and whether your role grants access. Do not share your login credentials to resolve an access issue.

### 2. Prepare for onboarding

Before your team begins, agree on who owns setup and who will use each workflow. Gather the product information, customer details, and opening stock records relevant to your launch. Decide which products need inventory tracking and how your staff should record corrections or other stock changes.

Start with a manageable set of records. Review imported or entered information for accuracy, including names, prices, tax settings, and active status. Assign staff only the access needed for their responsibilities, and give them a short walkthrough of the tasks they will perform.

### 3. Work with products

Use the product area to maintain the catalog used by sales and inventory workflows. Keep product names recognizable, prices current, and tax or stock-tracking settings aligned with your business process. Review records periodically so staff are not choosing from obsolete or duplicate items.

When importing products, prepare a clean source file and follow the fields and format presented by the import tool. Check a small sample after import before relying on the full catalog. Correct errors at their source where practical to reduce repeat cleanup.

### 4. Manage customers

Create or update customer records according to your organization's data practices. Use consistent naming and avoid creating duplicates when a customer already exists. Add only information needed to support the relationship and your business process. Follow your organization's privacy and retention requirements when handling personal information.

Customer records may be associated with sales activity. Reviewing the record and its related history can help staff understand prior interactions before assisting a returning customer.

### 5. Record a sale

At the point of sale, select the relevant products and quantities, review applicable pricing and promotions, and confirm the order details before completing the transaction. Associate a customer when appropriate. Record the payment using the methods enabled for your workspace and follow your organization's procedure for any exception.

After completion, review the order record if you need to confirm items, totals, payment activity, or refund history. If a transaction appears incorrect, follow the established correction or refund process rather than creating a second transaction to compensate without review.

### 6. Review stock activity

Stock levels are meaningful only when recorded activity is consistent. Use the inventory workflow to review stock-tracked products, examine changes, and identify items that may be low. When recording an adjustment, follow your organization's approval and reason-coding practices so the change can be understood later.

Investigate a difference by checking recent sales and stock movements, then confirm the physical count when appropriate. A low-stock indicator is a prompt to review, not a substitute for operational judgment or a purchase order.

### 7. Manage users and access

Workspace owners or authorized managers should invite and manage users according to the controls available in their account. Assign roles that reflect job responsibilities and review access when someone's role changes or they leave the organization. If you cannot see a setting, ask an authorized owner rather than sharing an account.

### 8. Understand subscriptions and apps

Plan and subscription information is managed through the platform's billing and administration processes. Your available apps may be tied to your subscription and configuration. Before changing a plan or enabling a module, confirm the effect on access, billing, and existing workflows with the account administrator or support contact.

### Troubleshooting checklist

- Confirm you are in the right organization and using the right account.
- Check whether your role includes the action you are trying to perform.
- Verify required product, customer, or order fields before submitting.
- Refresh the relevant record and check whether the action already completed.
- Note the app, approximate time, steps taken, and any displayed message.
- Contact support through the route provided for your account; omit passwords and sensitive customer data.

### Documentation feedback

If a guide is unclear or no longer matches your account, contact support with the page title and the step that needs attention. Clear feedback helps us keep instructions useful as the platform and its available apps evolve.
